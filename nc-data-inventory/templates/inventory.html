Data availability at the RCCP study sites

Each project site is matched to its nearest NOAA / USGS water-level gauge, NDBC wave buoy, wind station and USGS stream gauge, and to the nearest output of every model / hindcast. Ratings combine distance with record length, and are capped where an open-ocean source cannot represent waves or levels inside a sound or river.

Nearest station, distance and record length

Rating rules. Good / fair distance limits: water level ≤10 / ≤30 km, waves ≤25 / ≤60 km, wind ≤15 / ≤40 km, river flow ≤15 / ≤40 km. Records under 10 years (under 20 for water level, the minimum for annual-maxima EVA) are capped at fair. Open-ocean waves (NDBC buoys, WIS) and GTSM water levels are capped at fair at sound, estuary and river sites; CORA's ~500 m mesh resolves the sounds and is not capped. River flow is rated only at sites with a riverine or compound-flood hazard; elsewhere it is shown in grey. Overall is the mean of the rated categories (rounded half up); incl. models takes, per category, the better of observation and model. Model ratings describe spatial coverage (distance to the nearest output point / node / cell), not accuracy — check skill in the Validation tab.
